Node:Cue

One free show-control application for cues, pads, playlists, routing, fades, MIDI, OSC, keyboard shortcuts, and Stream Deck control.

Download latest release Get supporter license

Cues for the show order.

Build a precise sequence, launch it from transport controls, and let each item prepare the next step of the show.

  • Audio, stop, MIDI, and OSC cues in the same list.
  • Pre-wait, post-wait, play-next, loop, trim, fades, and crossfades.
  • Keep the armed cue visible even when the list is scrolled.

Pads for instant material.

Keep jingles, stingers, atmospheres, and one-shot sounds ready without leaving the main show interface.

  • Pages of pads with names, colors, images, loops, and re-click behavior.
  • Trigger from mouse, keyboard, MIDI, OSC, or Stream Deck.
  • Route pads to their own bus while cues and playlist keep playing.

Playlist for continuous playback.

Run a music bed or interval playlist while cues and pads stay available for live interventions.

  • Active tracks, trim-aware total duration, loop mode, overlap, and auto-trim.
  • Fade from a cue into the playlist, or fade the playlist out to return to cues.
  • Use a dedicated playlist bus for clean routing to another output device.

One workspace. Three buses.

Node:Cue is built for shows that need more than a linear list: fire a cue, trigger pads on top, crossfade into playlist music, and keep each family on a separate audio output.

CuesOutput 1 - main cue system
PadsOutput 2 - stingers and live hits
PlaylistOutput 3 - music bed or foyer feed
