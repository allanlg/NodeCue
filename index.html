Node:Cue

Audio playback for Windows, designed for live events.

Run a cue sequence, keep sounds ready on pads and manage background music with three connected players. Prepare the planned parts of a show and respond when things change.

Three players, one show.

Use the one that fits the moment. They stay connected, so each can respond to the others.

Build the planned sequence.

Arrange everything in the order it needs to run, with waits, fades and transitions where you need them.

  • Audio, Stop, MIDI, OSC, Go To, Time and Note cues
  • Groups played together, in sequence or as a sublist
  • Trim, fades, crossfades, EQ, compression and markers

Keep jingles ready to play.

Trigger jingles, stings and sound effects at any time, outside the planned sequence.

  • Pages, custom images, colors and hotkeys
  • One sound at a time, or several together
  • Global hotkeys while another application is in front

Manage the background music.

Play event ambience before, during and after the show, from tracks or an external source.

  • One playlist, or two side by side
  • Auto-trim, overlap, normalization and random order
  • Resume from the same place after an interruption
  • Auxiliary input with controlled transitions to tracks

Markers

Mark positions on the waveform, then go to them during the show from the keyboard, MIDI, OSC, Timecode or Stream Deck. Each marker starts playback or moves the playhead. Marker List brings every marker of a Cuelist together.

Dual Playlist

Open two playlists side by side, each with its own controls and output bus. Play them together, or let one crossfade into the other.

Is Node:Cue right for your workflow?

Who is Node:Cue designed for?

Node:Cue is designed for live sound engineers who need flexible audio playback for live events. Set up smooth, automatic transitions quickly without configuring every file individually, or fine-tune individual cues when the show calls for precise timing and detailed settings. It adapts to both tight schedules and carefully programmed productions.

Why combine a Cuelist, Pads and Playlists?

The three players work together through configurable transition rules. Launch a jingle from a Pad and fade out the background Playlist, then return to the Playlist afterwards. A Cuelist cue can stop the Playlist, or a Pad can interrupt Cuelist playback. Choose what stops, what fades and what keeps playing, so you can follow a planned sequence and react to the event as it happens.

Is Node:Cue an alternative to QLab?

Yes, for audio playback on Windows. Node:Cue grew from the need for a QLab-like audio tool on Windows, with a different approach shaped by live event workflows. Its connected Cuelist, Pads and Playlist players bring planned sequences, spontaneous sound triggers and background music into one application.

Node:Cue focuses on audio playback. It does not replace QLab for uses such as video playback or lighting control.

Can I open my existing projects in 1.2?

Yes. Node:Cue 1.2 opens projects from earlier versions and keeps a copy of their previous state the first time they are saved. A project saved with 1.2 can no longer be opened in 1.1.
